# Roadmap

Waymark is testing one thesis: saved information becomes valuable when it is
easy to retrieve and turn into action.

## Now — prove the rescue loop

- Measure whether people find known saves in under 30 seconds.
- Improve first-run guidance and browser export instructions.
- Test retrieval with vague memories, intentions, and topics.
- Make duplicate and forgotten-save explanations clearer.
- Strengthen accessibility and keyboard navigation.

## Next — make it portable

- Export and re-import a portable Waymark library.
- Add local notes, status, and lightweight collections.
- Support additional bookmark formats.
- Add opt-in browser-extension capture.
- Make action packs editable and shareable without exposing the full library.

## Later — trusted assistance

- On-device or explicitly consented semantic search.
- User-controlled connectors for reading lists and saved social posts.
- Local-first sync with clear encryption and deletion guarantees.
- A plugin system for community-built importers and actions.

## Not planned

- Advertising based on browsing history.
- Selling or silently uploading bookmark data.
- Requiring an account for the core local workflow.
- Gamifying the number of links saved.
